# How to Learn Quickly

The key is learn it slowly. Balancing your theory intake rate with their practice and habit forming rate.

[TOC]

Here`s some tips you can follow:

- balance your theory and practice

  > [!TIP]
  >
  > Normally, one hour theory meets five hours practice. The time is not that  important, check when you form the new habit.

- how to check your new habit: when you can feel that things are becoming easier and faster without compromising your accuracy and your consistency.
